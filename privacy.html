OpenBike

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

OpenBike ("the app") is a bike navigation app for Singapore. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: OpenBike has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we don't collect or sell your personal data.

Your location

With your permission, OpenBike uses your device's location to show where you are on the map, to use as the start of a route, and to guide you turn-by-turn. Location is only used while the app is open. We don't store your location history. You can turn location access off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Services the app talks to

To work, the app sends some information to the following services. They receive your device's IP address as part of any internet request, and may keep standard server logs under their own policies.

  • Route planning: the start, end and any stop coordinates of your route, and your current position if you go off route, are sent to a Valhalla routing server (currently the public server operated by FOSSGIS e.V.) to calculate the route.
  • Address search: the text you type in the search boxes is sent to OneMap, operated by the Singapore Land Authority, to find matching places.
  • Map display: map images for the area you're viewing are loaded from OpenFreeMap, and the map software is loaded from the unpkg content network.
  • App updates: the app checks Expo's update service for bug fixes and improvements, which involves sending basic technical details such as the app version.

None of these requests include your name, email or any account details, because OpenBike doesn't ask for them.

Data stored on your phone

Your saved places (Home and favourites) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded and are deleted if you delete the app.

Children

OpenBike doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
