OpenBike

Bike navigation for Singapore and the US

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

  • Routes tuned to your bike: Road, Commuter, Gravel or E-Bike
  • Choose Fastest or Fewest Stops, and route styles like Bike Paths First (PCN-First in Singapore) or Avoid Main Roads
  • See how much of your ride is bike path, bike lane, shared path or road
  • Turn-by-turn voice navigation with automatic rerouting
  • Round trips, plus one-tap routing to Home and up to 5 favourite places

OpenBike is available on the App Store for iPhone.
