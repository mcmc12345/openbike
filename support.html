Common questions

Which countries does OpenBike cover?
Singapore and the United States. South Korea is coming soon, and more countries are planned.

How do I switch between miles and kilometres?
OpenBike follows your phone's settings. On iPhone: Settings → General → Language & Region → Measurement System. On Android, it follows your phone's region.

Why isn't navigation following me?
Allow location access for OpenBike. On iPhone: Settings → OpenBike → Location → "While Using the App". On Android: Settings → Apps → OpenBike → Permissions → Location → "Allow only while using the app". Keep the app open on screen while you navigate.

Can I try navigation without riding?
Yes. Plan any route and tap Demo ride to watch the turn-by-turn guidance play through.

What does "Keep to marked cycling lane" mean?
Your route uses shared paths, sidewalks or park connectors. Ride considerately, keep to the cycling side where marked, and give way to pedestrians.

Where are my saved places stored?
Only on your phone. They're removed if you delete the app.

Safety

Routes are suggestions based on open map data and may not reflect current conditions, closures or construction. Always follow road signs, traffic laws and local cycling rules, and use your own judgement.
